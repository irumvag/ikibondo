# Glossary for translators

One agreed word for each term, used on every screen, SMS and email (`docs/i18n.md`, "Ground rules").
Fill in the Kinyarwanda and French columns before translating `frontend/messages/en.json`.

**A native Kinyarwanda speaker with health-worker experience writes or approves every entry.** Where the
health centres already use a word (on the vaccination card, in Ministry of Health or UNHCR materials, or what
CHWs say during visits), use that word, and note where it comes from.

Columns: **Term** as it appears in the English text · **What it means in Ikibondo** · **Kinyarwanda** ·
**French** · **Notes** (source, or "keep in English").
